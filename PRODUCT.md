# PRODUCT.md — Fennec

## Register
**product** — app UI / tool. Design serves the task; the interface should
disappear into the work. (Marketing surfaces like the landing live in a
separate repo, `fennec-landing`, with their own register.)

## Users & Purpose
Independent music producers and composers building a career — on their
phone, between studio sessions, often at night. Fennec is their business
hub: price work and send quotes, get honest feedback on tracks, plan
content, watch their numbers (Fennec dB), and build their network.

The job on any given screen: *move my music career forward in the two
minutes I have right now.*

## Brand Personality
**Quiet · precise · warm-analog.** Minimal Apple discipline with the soul
of a studio object — tape, grease pencil, amber VU-glow. Confidence
through restraint, warmth through craft, never through noise.

## Anti-references
- SoundCloud-style social noise: like counts, fire emojis, comment spam.
- Streaming-player clichés: waveform bars as decoration, DJ-neon gradients.
- Gamification confetti: badges raining, streak guilt, duolingo-owl energy.
  (Karma is an economy, not a slot machine.)

## Strategic Design Principles
1. **Amber = human presence.** The accent marks where a person acted —
   never decoration, never progress fills. Color density = conversation density.
2. **English-only UI**, always — even though the maker chats in Spanish.
3. **The daily loop is the product**: listen → mark → get sealed → upload.
   Every surface should shorten the path back into that loop.
4. **No scroll where a glance should do** (Dashboard is a fixed instrument
   panel, not a feed).
5. **PWA-first on iOS**: safe-areas, standalone viewport quirks, and
   one-hand reach are structural constraints, not afterthoughts.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body contrast on dark surfaces, reduced-motion alternatives
for every animation (already standard in the Feedback module), touch
targets ≥ 40px.
